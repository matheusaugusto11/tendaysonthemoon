# Campo "Use of Super Intelligence (SI)": texto para o formulário

Texto em inglês para colar no campo **Use of Super Intelligence (SI)** da submissão (ver
[SUBMISSAO.md](SUBMISSAO.md), campo 6). Segundo a NASA, essa resposta **não vai para os juízes**, é só coleta de dados.
O que vale para os juízes são as marcas d'água, o texto da página e os metadados, que já estão declarados
([USO-DE-IA.md](../USO-DE-IA.md), tela de créditos, rodapé do jogo e página do itch.io).

O texto abaixo foi escrito a partir do que o repositório mostra. O time confirmou em 15/11/2026 que as **únicas
ferramentas de IA usadas foram Claude e Gemini**. Se isso mudar (outra ferramenta, ou som e música gerados por IA),
acrescentar ao texto e ao [USO-DE-IA.md](../USO-DE-IA.md).

---

## Texto para colar

```
Yes. AI was used as an assistant. The idea of the game and how it answers the challenge came from the team, not from AI.

Idea and design (human): the ten-day mission, the energy-cell trade-off (6 cells for 10 slots), the choice to teach space weather through consequences, and the selection of NASA data (DONKI flares, SEP and CME events, OSDR OSD-268, and the ISS/Artemis task pages) were decided by the team. The NASA data was copied from the cited NASA pages and catalog. It was not generated by AI.

Code (Claude, by Anthropic): parts of the C# game code in Unity and the automated tests (EditMode) were written with AI assistance and reviewed and tested by the team. The Blender Python scripts used to build the 3D station and characters (ArtSource/EstacaoRoda/fonte) were also written with AI assistance.

Images (Gemini, by Google): concept boards and reference images of the station, characters and UI screens were generated with AI and used only as visual references to model the final 3D assets in Blender. No AI-generated image is shown inside the game. AI-generated images in the repository and in the demo carry a visible "AI-GENERATED IMAGE" or "AI-assisted art" label. The final game art (3D models, icons, textures) was built from the team's own Blender models and Unity assets.

Text (Claude): some in-game texts and the English/Portuguese translations were first drafted with AI and then reviewed and edited by the team.

Audio: not generated by AI. Five sound effects come from Pixabay (Pixabay Content License) and the other interface sounds are synthesized in code.

AI use is also declared in the project page (itch.io), the in-game Credits screen, the in-game footer and USO-DE-IA.md in the repository.
```

## Confirmado pelo time (15/11/2026)

Ferramentas de IA usadas: **Claude** (código, testes, scripts do Blender, rascunho de textos e traduções) e
**Gemini** (conceitos e imagens de referência). Nenhuma outra. A divisão por tarefa vem do repositório: o time
confirmou as duas ferramentas, não cada uso. Se o Gemini também rascunhou textos, trocar "Claude" por "Claude and
Gemini" na linha "Text".
