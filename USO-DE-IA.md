# Use of AI / Uso de IA

## English

The **idea and design of the game** (the energy-cell trade-off, the ten-day mission, the choice of
NASA data) come from the team. AI tools were used as assistants, not to invent how to solve the challenge.

| Area | AI use | Notice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Code | Parts of the C# code and the tests were written with AI assistance (Claude) and reviewed by the team. The Blender Python scripts in `ArtSource/EstacaoRoda/fonte` were also written with AI assistance. | Stated here, in the in-game credits and in the submission form. |
| Art and concepts | Concept boards and references were generated with AI (Gemini) and used to model the station and characters in Blender. | No AI-generated image is loaded by the game. AI-generated images in the repository (`Docs/conceitos`, `Docs/image-ref`, `ArtSource`, `Assets/refs`) carry a **visible AI label**, and so does any AI image shown in the demo or the project page. |
| Text | Some texts and translations were drafted with AI and reviewed by the team. | Stated here and in the in-game credits. |
| Data | NASA data was **not** generated by AI. It was copied from the DONKI catalog and the pages cited in `Docs/DADOS-NASA.md`. | |

## Português

A **ideia e o desenho do jogo** vieram da equipe. A IA foi usada como assistente: código e testes
(Claude), conceitos e imagens de referência (Gemini), rascunho de textos e traduções.
Toda imagem gerada por IA que apareça no jogo, na demo ou na página precisa de **marca d'água visível**.
Os dados da NASA não foram gerados por IA.

> A equipe confirmou em 15/11/2026 que as únicas ferramentas de IA usadas foram **Claude** e **Gemini**
> (texto do formulário em `Docs/CAMPO-USO-DE-IA.md`). As imagens de IA do repositório já têm marca visível.
